# Inventory Manager

A mobile-first inventory management app for a tea & café business. Staff log daily stock movements (in/out), the boss monitors low-stock alerts for restocking, and month-end physical counts are reconciled against system totals.

**Who it's for:** Internal team — staff for daily stock logging, boss/manager for reorder decisions and audits.

**Core workflows:**
- Quick stock in/out logging with category → item → quantity → reason flow
- Master inventory list with instant search and category filtering
- Low stock alert page showing items below reorder thresholds with CSV export
- Month-end physical count reconciliation with automatic variance adjustments
